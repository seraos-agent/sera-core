# Data Deletion Instructions

According to the Meta Platform rules, you have the right to request the deletion of your personal data collected by SERA through the Threads API.

## How to Disconnect and Delete Your Data

**Step 1: Revoke Access via Meta**
You can remove our app's access to your Threads/Instagram account directly from your Instagram settings:
1. Open the Instagram app and navigate to **Settings and activity**.
2. Tap on **Apps and Websites**.
3. Under the **Active** tab, locate **Sera** and tap **Remove**.

**Step 2: Request Full Data Wipe from SERA**
To completely wipe any historical data, insights, or OAuth tokens stored on SERA's servers:
1. Send an email to **seraos.agent@gmail.com**.
2. Use the subject line: `Data Deletion Request`.
3. Include your Threads username in the body of the email.

We will process your request and permanently delete all your data (including encrypted tokens and fetched mentions) from our database within 72 hours. We will reply to your email to confirm that the deletion is complete.
