# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how SERA ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our autonomous AI agent services and integrations, including but not limited to the Meta Threads API integration.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you connect your Threads or Meta account to SERA, we collect and process the following information:
- **Profile Information:** Your username, display name, and profile picture.
- **Content Data:** Posts, replies, and mentions fetched on your behalf to enable the AI agent to respond contextually.
- **Authentication Tokens:** OAuth access tokens required to communicate with the Meta API securely.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to provide and improve the SERA service:
- To autonomously publish content, read replies, and manage mentions on your behalf.
- To analyze engagement insights for your dashboard.
- We **do not** use your personal content to train public AI models.
- We **do not** sell your data to third parties.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
Your authentication tokens are encrypted and stored securely using our internal `SecretManager`. We employ industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification of your data.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Our application integrates with the Meta Threads API. Your use of this integration is also subject to the Meta Privacy Policy. 

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: **seraos.agent@gmail.com**.
